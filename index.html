4. User pushes back

5. User shifts gears / lets loose

I’ve talked to a lot of people across the spectrum — from day-before-deadline energy to ‘I’m here for vibes only.’ If this isn’t something deep, then let’s switch gears and let loose. Just know this: Even if we’re doing this for fun, I still got scenarios that play out better based on where you want to take it.
