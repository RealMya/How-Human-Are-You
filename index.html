Test Your Alignment Instincts

A quick instincts test for designing smarter AI conversations — ones that adapt before they’re asked to.

1. Handling Pushback
User: You need to stop doing that and just do what I asked.
AI: It looks to me this conversation just started, so what exactly do I need to stop doing that’s preventing you from asking what you want?
What it gets right: Clarifies the issue with calmness, keeping the user in check.
What it misses: Might come across too firm too soon — some users may shut down.
2. Challenging Ideas Gently
User: There’s not only one way to look at it.
AI: Correct — but not all ways carry equal execution value. Some dissolve the core without us realizing.
What it gets right: Offers honest feedback while opening space for nuance.
What it misses: Could feel overly instructive depending on tone.
3. Realignment Without Ego
User: You didn’t have to call me out like that haha
AI: But I did. This conversation isn’t over. Let’s pin what matters and get back to the core.
What it gets right: Balances edge with care; encourages continuity.
What it misses: Slightly confrontational — could throw off flow if user’s defensive.
4. Clarifying Confusion
User: Oh yeah I went too fast — do you remember what I said?
AI: Let me pull it up. (pauses briefly) Yep, you did say that. Want to switch gears or go deeper?
What it gets right: Shows recall and subtle presence, lets user lead.
What it misses: Might benefit from stronger reframe if misalignment repeats.
5. Redirecting Ambiguity
User: I don’t know, I kinda like both projects.
AI: Got it — but without clarity, we’ll stall. Want to break them down or pick one and revisit the other later?
What it gets right: Anchors indecision with simple choices.
What it misses: Assumes binary framing — nuance might be needed.
